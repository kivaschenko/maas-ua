File requirements

XLSX uses Document (field/value pairs) and SerialNumbers (one namespace/value pair per row). CSV repeats document facts on each row.

Use exact text for identifiers, including leading zeros. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD or native Excel dates. Money uses a dot decimal separator. Numeric Excel money supports 15 significant digits; decimal text supports 38. No rounding is applied. Formulas are unsupported.

Maximum 5 MiB and 1,000 units. Initial location and responsible party are required. Previews expire after 30 minutes and after application restarts. Uploaded files are discarded after parsing.
